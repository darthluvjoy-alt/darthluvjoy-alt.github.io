Short description

Eclipse Relics is a free-to-play fantasy RPG where players collect heroes, build a squad and fight turn-based battles. Choose a starter hero, develop skills and gear, and take on campaign stages and bosses.

The setting

The Eclipse has shattered the realm. Build your team and fight to reclaim the light.
