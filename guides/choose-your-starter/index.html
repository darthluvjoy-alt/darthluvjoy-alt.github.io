Eclipse Relics / Beginner guides

Your first hero

Lyra, Niamh or Ignaris: who should you choose?

Your adventure begins with one of three starter heroes. Here is a quick introduction to their classes and leadership styles.

Archer

Lyra

A woodland archer. Her leader ability, Hawk’s Eye, supports her other allies’ Attack and Speed.

Consider Lyra if you like the archer fantasy and want to build around an offensive leadership bonus.

Rogue

Niamh

A former Tempest cultist with twin daggers. Tempest Veil supports her other allies’ Defense and Speed.

Consider Niamh if you like the rogue fantasy and want leadership with a defensive component.

Bladedancer

Ignaris

A high-elf bladedancer with a fire-lit sword. His leader ability, Kindled Edge, supports his other allies’ Attack and Speed.

Consider Ignaris if you like the bladedancer fantasy and attack-focused leadership.

A leader’s bonus applies while that hero is leading. These three leadership abilities support the other allies in the squad. Check the current in-game descriptions when choosing your leader.

Choose for the team you want to build.

You choose one starter; the choice screen does not award all three. Your other heroes, gear and the encounter all matter, so there is no single recommendation here for every future team.

Read the starter’s abilities, choose the character you want to play, and learn how that hero works with the rest of your squad.

Your first-session checklist

  1. Follow the prologue and tutorials. They introduce battle controls, hero development and the game’s menus.
  2. Check the squad before entering a stage. Make sure the heroes you intend to use are actually selected.
  3. Read the abilities. Look at what each available skill does and how the leader bonus supports the team.
  4. Review your active team’s upgrades. Check their levels, skills and equipped gear before a difficult encounter.
  5. Work towards the first chapter boss. Use The Alpha preparation checklist when you reach stage 1-10.
