Chapter one · Stage 1-10

Get your squad ready for The Alpha.

The Alpha is the wolf boss at the end of chapter one. Watch a three-hero squad take it on, then check your own team before the fight.

Watch the 17-second fight on YouTube

Play on Android Get it on iOS

1. Check who is actually in your squad.

Review the selected team before entering the stage. An upgraded hero only helps if that hero is in the squad you take into battle.

2. Choose a leader for the whole team.

Read the leader ability and consider the other heroes it supports. The starter leaders have different combinations of Attack, Defense and Speed support. See the starter guide for an introduction.

3. Review the heroes you are using.

Open your active heroes and check their levels, skills and equipped gear. Before spreading your available resources across more heroes, consider which upgrades help the squad you are taking into this encounter.

4. Understand your skill choices.

If an attempt on Auto is not working, try switching Auto off to choose the available abilities yourself. Read what the skills do and pay attention to the timing of the battle.

5. Learn from the attempt.

Notice which ally falls first, which abilities you had available, and what changed when you adjusted the team. Use that information for the next attempt rather than changing everything at once.

This is a preparation checklist. Your roster, gear and upgrades affect the fight. It is not a guaranteed-win team or a fixed minimum-level recommendation.

Share your own approach.

When discussing an attempt, share the game’s name, the squad you used and a short explanation of your hero choices. That helps other players who are building their first team.

If you would like Klaam Games to consider featuring your gameplay, contact us by email. Tell us what you recorded and which parts, if any, we may share with credit. Keep account IDs, receipts and private information out of public clips.
